How to play

1. Pick the finish. Tap the board where you want the bag to end up (green).

2. Pick the landing. Drag the yellow ring around the gold zone. Near the green spot it drops and sticks; farther back it comes in flat and slides. Off to the side adds spin.

3. Throw. Tap THROW, then pull back anywhere. The bar fills with power and the arrow swings, faster the harder you pull. Let go with the bar in the gold band and the arrow pointing straight. Pull into OVERDRIVE and the throw goes wild.

Wind. The aiming guide ignores wind. Check the wind arrow and aim off to allow for it.

Opponents. PLAY looks for a live player at your court. When no one is available, you'll play a computer opponent.

Career. Both players put in the entry and the winner takes the pot. A win at the Backyard is +2 stars and a loss is −1; each venue up adds one to both. Quitting a match counts as a loss. Stars unlock new venues with bigger pots, slicker boards, more wind and tougher opponents. Spend coins on bags with better accuracy, control and spin, and on board designs. Short on coins? The Backyard entry is on the house.

Scoring. In the hole is 3, on the board is 1. Only the difference counts: throw 4 to their 1 and you score 3. First to 21 wins.

Tip: in Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen, to open this like an app. Your career is saved on this device.
