Privacy Policy

Cornhole Throwdown · Effective November 20, 2026

Cornhole Throwdown ("the game", "we", "us") is a cornhole game for iPhone and iPad. This policy explains what information the game uses and why. In short: we don't collect any personal information, we don't show ads, and we don't track you.

Information we collect

None. The game has no accounts of its own, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. It does not send any information to us or to anyone acting for us.

Information stored on your device and in your Apple account

  • Your game progress (coins, stars, bags, boards and settings) is saved on your device. If you're signed in to iCloud and Game Center, Apple also keeps a copy in your iCloud and Game Center account so your progress follows you to a new iPhone or iPad. Apple stores this under Apple's own privacy policy; we can't see it.
  • Your player name. The game gives you a "guest" name, or you can pick your own. If you're signed in to Game Center, your Game Center name and picture may be shown instead.

Game Center

The game uses Apple's Game Center for leaderboards, seasons and live matches. Your Game Center name, scores and the moves you make in a live match are shared with Apple and shown to other players through Game Center. Game Center is run by Apple under the Apple Privacy Policy. You can turn Game Center off, hide your profile, or limit who can play with you in the Settings app (Game Center, and Screen Time > Content & Privacy Restrictions for children).

In-app purchases

Purchases are handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never see your name, payment details or Apple Account. The game only learns which item was bought so it can deliver it. Coins, bottle caps and other in-game items have no cash value and can't be exchanged for real money. Parents can turn off or require approval for purchases with Screen Time and Family Sharing (Ask to Buy).

Notifications

If you allow it, the game schedules reminders on your device (for example, when a Prize Can is ready). These are created on your device; no information is sent to us. You can turn them off in the game's settings or in the Settings app.

Network time

To keep in-game timers fair, the game checks the current time with an Apple web server. Only a standard web request is made; nothing about you is sent.

Children

The game is suitable for children. We don't knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13. Because the game collects no personal information, there is nothing for us to share or sell. Parents can control Game Center, multiplayer and purchases through Screen Time and Family Sharing on the child's device.

Your choices and your data

Since we hold no information about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete. To erase your progress, delete the game from your device, and remove its data from iCloud in Settings > [your name] > iCloud > Manage Account Storage.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update this page and the date at the top.
