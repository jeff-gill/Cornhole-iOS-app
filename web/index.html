How to play

1. Pick the finish. Tap the board where you want the bag to end up (green).

2. Pick the landing. Drag the yellow ring around the gold zone. Near the green spot it drops and sticks; farther back it comes in flat and slides. Off to the side adds spin so it hooks toward the finish.

3. Pull back. Tap THROW, then drag down anywhere and let go when the power bar is in the gold band. Too far is long, not enough is short.

4. Hit the ring. Tap to stop the left-right needle, then the up-down needle. The closer to the centre, the straighter and truer the arc.

Push shots. If one of your own bags is in the way, the game throws a bit harder so yours shoves it along your line.

Scoring. In the hole is 3, on the board is 1. Only the difference counts: throw 4 to their 1 and you score 3. First to 21 wins.
