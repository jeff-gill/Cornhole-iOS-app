How to play

1. Mark your spots. Tap the board where the bag should land (yellow). Tap again where it should finish (green). Drag either marker to adjust. Stick it puts both on the same spot for a high, dead-drop throw.

2. The game plans the perfect throw. Flatter throws come in fast and slide. Higher throws drop steep and stick.

3. Stop three needles. Direction pushes it left or right, Strength sends it long or short, and Height changes the arc, so it lands somewhere else and slides a different amount. Only three perfect stops go exactly where you planned.

Scoring. In the hole is 3, on the board is 1. Only the difference counts each inning. First to 21 wins. Knock your opponent's bags off, or knock your own in.
