How to play

1. Pick the finish. Tap where you want the bag to end up (green). A dotted line shows your throw from your hand to that spot. Tap anywhere else to move it.

2. Pick the landing. Drag the yellow ring around the gold zone to choose where the bag hits first. Land it close to the finish for a high arc that sticks, or farther back for a flat throw that slides in. Stick it drops the bag right on the finish.

3. Add spin. Move the ring off the centre line and the bag gets spin, so it kicks and hooks toward the finish after landing. A longer slide has more room to hook, so the zone gets wider farther back.

4. Stop three needles. Direction pushes it left or right, Strength sends it long or short, and Height changes the arc, so it lands somewhere else and slides a different amount. Only three perfect stops go exactly where you planned.

Scoring. In the hole is 3, on the board is 1. Only the difference counts: if you throw 4 and they throw 1, you score 3 and they score nothing. First to 21 wins. Knock your opponent's bags off, or knock your own in.
