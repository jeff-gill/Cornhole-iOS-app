How to play

1. Pick the finish. Tap the board where you want the bag to end up (green).

2. Pick the landing. Drag the yellow ring around the gold zone. Near the green spot it drops and sticks; farther back it comes in flat and slides. Off to the side adds spin.

3. Throw. Tap THROW, then pull back anywhere. The bar fills with power and the arrow swings, faster the harder you pull. Let go with the bar in the gold band and the arrow pointing straight. Pull into OVERDRIVE and the throw goes wild.

Scoring. In the hole is 3, on the board is 1. Only the difference counts: throw 4 to their 1 and you score 3. First to 21 wins. Whoever scores in an inning throws first in the next. After a wash, the same side leads again.

Wind. The aiming guide ignores wind. Check the wind arrow and aim off to allow for it.

Career. Both players put in the buy-in and the winner takes the pot. A win earns stars at that court and a loss costs some (the court card shows how many). Fill a court's star track to open the next one.

Opponents. PLAY looks for a live player at your court. When no one is available, you'll play a computer opponent.

Tip: in Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen, to open this like an app. Your career is saved on this device.

Cornhole rules

The standard rules of cornhole, as played in leagues and tournaments.

🪵 The court

  • Two boards face each other, 27 feet apart, measured between their front edges.
  • Each board is 2 feet wide and 4 feet long, with a 6-inch hole centred 9 inches from the top.
  • The front of the board sits about 3 to 4 inches off the ground and the back about 12 inches.

🎒 The bags

  • Each side has four bags of its own colour.
  • A bag is 6 inches square and weighs about a pound.

👟 Throwing

  • You throw from the pitcher's box, the area to the left or right of the board.
  • Stay in your box and behind the front edge of the board (the foul line) until the bag leaves your hand.
  • Throw underhand. Players take turns, one bag at a time, until all eight bags are thrown. That's one inning.
  • In singles both players throw from the same end. In doubles, partners stand at opposite ends.
  • Whoever scored in the last inning throws first in the next. After a wash, the same side throws first again.

🎯 Scoring

  • A bag in the hole (a "cornhole") is worth 3 points. That includes a bag knocked in by another bag.
  • A bag resting on the board is worth 1 point. A bag hanging over the edge of the hole is still 1 point until it falls in. In this game, a bag that's more than half over the hole falls in.
  • A bag that touches the ground, or hits the ground and bounces onto the board, scores nothing and is taken off.
  • Points cancel out: only the difference between the two sides counts each inning. Equal scores are a wash.

🏆 Winning

  • The first side to reach 21 points at the end of an inning wins.

🚫 Fouls

  • Stepping past the foul line, throwing from outside your box or throwing out of turn is a foul. A fouled bag scores nothing and is taken off the board.
  • Taking too long to throw can also be called as a foul in leagues.
